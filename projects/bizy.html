BIZY: Faceless Business-Story Shorts

PausedVideo editingStorytelling

A faceless YouTube channel of 45 to 90 second stories about how well-known companies made famous decisions, made entirely on a phone.

What I made

  • Five Shorts between August 22 and 25, 2026 (Costco's $1.50 hot dog, Airbnb's cereal boxes, Blockbuster's missed deal, In-N-Out's slow growth and Domino's turnaround)
  • A repeatable pipeline: script, text-to-speech voice, images, edit, captions, thumbnail, upload
  • Cut production time from about 6 hours for the first video to under an hour by the fifth

Results I recorded

  • The Costco video reached about 18.7K views with roughly 74% of viewers staying, and brought in 26 subscribers
  • The next video got about 1.1K views, and the third only 18
  • What predicted success was how many people stayed to watch, not the posting time

Where it stands

I haven't added newer videos since the first week of school. The bottleneck I found was finding images that actually tell the story, so the fix I planned was a visual plan before editing and self-made graphics.

Statlete: Analytics for Youth Sports Coaches

ValidatingStartupAIProduct design

An app that takes data from athletes and turns it into in-depth analytics with AI and math, so coaches have one place to see how every player is developing. I'm co-CEO, technical co-lead and graphic design lead on a three-person team.

The product idea

  • Progress reports, goal setting and physical measures
  • Skill training and tracking
  • AI video analysis of practices and games, from an ordinary smartphone video
  • A profile and avatar for every player
  • Calendar and app integrations

What would make it different

  • Player cards, stats and levelling in the style of a video game, with shareable highlights, so players want to use it and tell others
  • No special hardware or expensive software, because phone video is enough
  • An athlete-owned "passport" of their data that follows them between clubs and programs, instead of staying with a team

Competition

Hudl is the established tool, and I see it as complex and expensive for a small team or coach. I haven't researched all competitors yet.

Where it stands

  • Brand and logo done (blue with black and white, kept minimal)
  • Right now we're finishing a short form and an email to send to coaches we can reach through our personal networks, so we learn what coaches actually need before we build more
  • The first raw MVP is my job and is not built yet

Why validate first

Coach feedback comes before the code, so we build what coaches actually need.

CARE: AI Skincare Guidance

Landing page onlyReactTypeScriptVite

An app idea that helps people understand their skin: describe or scan a problem, get the likely cause and a routine, and only then see product suggestions. The positioning is clarity first, not selling products.

What's built

  • A React + TypeScript + Vite coming-soon site with a three-step waitlist: email, an optional short survey, then a thank-you
  • Waitlist answers are sent to a Google Apps Script endpoint
  • A brand direction of a technology company, not a spa: white page, black navbar and a blue accent

How the idea changed

  • Dropped dropshipping because of returns, refunds and liability, and moved toward affiliate links
  • Moved toward a free tier with limited scans and a paid tier
  • Decided the app must never diagnose, and should point people to a doctor for warning signs

Where it stands

Only the landing page exists. There is no app. I haven't recorded how many people signed up.

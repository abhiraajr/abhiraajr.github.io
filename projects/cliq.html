CLIQ: 3D-Printed Key Clicker Fidgets

Selling3D printingProduct designApps Script

I design, 3D print and sell clicker fidgets built around keyboard switches, in 1x1 up to 1x4 key bars and a choice of colors. First sales were in April 2026, and I sell them at school.

What I built

  • Several housing, keycap and spring iterations, including a parametric spring generator where I logged thickness, spacing, width and coil-count trials to tune the click feel
  • An order builder web page (three versions): pick base color and keycap colors, add a note, see the subtotal and get a confirmation. A matching Google Apps Script project handles orders

What the sales taught me

  • The 1x4 sells best by far. It covers the four fingers that line up on a hand, and I pick it myself.
  • Assembly takes under a minute, but each piece takes about 30 minutes to print, and I can't leave the printer running while I'm at school. Print time is the bottleneck.
  • Profit per unit is only cents at my first prices, so the next step is testing a higher price on the 1x4.

Plan

  • Sell only 1x1 to 1x4 with white keycaps and six base colors
  • Keep a small stock printed ahead so sales don't wait on the printer
  • Track every sale and ask each buyer what they'd want in a new version

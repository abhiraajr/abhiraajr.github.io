Chestnut: Group Food Orders for School Fundraisers

Awaiting approvalStartupLogisticsWeb app

I'm co-founding Chestnut. The first version let students pre-order food that arrived in one scheduled drop. My school said no, so we reworked the model instead of quitting.

What changed

  • We read through the school district's food-sale rules and looked at how academic booster clubs in nearby counties work
  • New model: the school's academic booster runs the fundraiser and is the public face. They collect orders and money and keep the profit.
  • Chestnut handles the backend: buying from the restaurant, secure delivery and a small per-order fee or a flat fee
  • Pickup happens after the school day, with a QR code handoff so only students and staff get food

Where it stands

We emailed the school's academic booster team to ask for approval, and we're waiting on an answer. Nothing launches until we get one. There is no code yet. The plan is a simple website first, then a pre-launch signup list of 50 to 100 people to show real demand.

What I learned

  • A good idea can still be blocked by a rule. Reading the actual rules took the idea from "rejected" to "possible".
  • Who the customer is matters: the booster club, not the student, is who we have to convince first.
